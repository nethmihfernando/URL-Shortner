Frequently asked questions

How does a URL shortener work?

It stores your long URL under a short random code. When someone opens the short link, they are redirected to the original URL and the visit is counted.

Do short links expire?

No. A link works until you delete it.

Can I change where a link points?

Yes. Open a link's details, choose Edit destination, and save. The short link stays the same.

Why can't I see my old links?

The list is saved in this browser only. Use Find a short code to load any link by its code.
